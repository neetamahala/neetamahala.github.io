Neeta Mahala

Ph.D. Student in Civil Engineering
University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign

I study sustainable pavement materials and the use of quarry by-product fines in Otta seal surfacing for local and low-volume roads. My work combines pavement materials research, experimental performance evaluation, and data-driven methods to support practical, cost-effective transportation infrastructure.

Before joining UIUC, I spent nearly nine years with the National Highways Authority of India (NHAI), where I worked on highway construction, project monitoring, road safety, and procurement for major highway and expressway projects.

Research Interests

My research interests include sustainable pavement materials, Otta seal surfacing, quarry by-product reuse, pavement performance evaluation, transportation infrastructure, and data-driven pavement engineering. I am also interested in applying machine learning and computer vision to infrastructure monitoring and performance assessment.
