Neeta Mahala About Research Publications Experience Education Awards Skills CV Neeta Mahala I am a Ph.D. student in Civil and Environmental Engineering at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Prof. Erol Tutumluer. My current research focuses on using quarry by-product fines in Otta seal surfacing for local roads, with the goal of developing sustainable and cost-effective road-surfacing solutions. Before joining UIUC, I worked at the National Highways Authority of India (NHAI), Government of India, from 2015 to 2024, serving as Deputy Manager (Technical), Manager, and Deputy General Manager. My work included highway construction and project monitoring, pavement and bridge infrastructure, road-safety audits, accident-blackspot mitigation, and tendering and procurement for major highway projects. I earned my B.Tech. in Civil Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi.
